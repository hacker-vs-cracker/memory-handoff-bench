# Licensing map

Memory Handoff Bench contains code, documentation, aggregate research results,
and sanitized validation derivatives. They are licensed by path and material
type rather than by one blanket license.

## Code

Original source code, SQL, configuration, and executable scripts are licensed
under the Apache License 2.0 in `LICENSE`.

This generally covers code-bearing files under `protocols/` and any future
author-created scripts, except where an individual file states otherwise.

## Documentation and research derivatives

Author-created documentation, aggregate tables, analysis outputs, and sanitized
human-validation derivatives are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International, whose legal code is in `LICENSE-DATA.txt`.

This generally covers `README.md`, `PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_SCOPE.md`, `docs/`,
`results/`, `analysis/`, `human-validation/`, and `reproducibility/`, except
where an individual file states otherwise.

## Exclusions

The licenses do not override third-party licenses or terms. Model and provider
names remain the property of their owners. Third-party dependencies retain
their own licenses. Any model-generated material or external material included
for research documentation remains subject to applicable provider terms and
rights. Private off-repository evidence is not licensed or distributed merely
because its hash appears in the public integrity ledger.
